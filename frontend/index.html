Car Damage Detector

Local dev tool for the phase 2 detector. Upload a photo, it's sent to the backend at http://localhost:8000, and any detected damage boxes are drawn over the image.

Pre-labeled images from backend/batch_prelabel.py, shown least-confident first. Edit the boxes, then "Save & next" to store the correction and move on -- nothing advances automatically without that click.

Detections

    Drag a box to move it, drag a corner handle to resize it, click the × to delete it. Use "Draw new box" to add damage the model missed, then "Save correction" (or "Save & next" in the review queue) to store the current boxes as training data.
